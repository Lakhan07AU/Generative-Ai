Assignment 8 – Short Observations

1. Similar digits form recognizable clusters in the 2D latent space, although some classes overlap.
2. The clusters are partially separated rather than perfectly separated because the VAE is unsupervised and uses only two latent dimensions.
3. Moving gradually through the latent space changes the generated digits gradually, with shape, stroke direction, thickness, and curvature morphing between neighbouring regions.
4. The transitions are generally smooth. Some intermediate outputs become blurry or ambiguous, which is expected between digit classes.
